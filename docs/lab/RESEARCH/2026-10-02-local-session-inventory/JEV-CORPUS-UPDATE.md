**Cursor database visibility:** The local Cursor `conversation-search.db` index has 702 local conversation rows and 430 cloud-run rows with `bc-*` IDs. Of the 385 unique parent JSONL IDs, 380 exactly match local index IDs; none match the cloud-run IDs. No `bc-*` parent JSONL was found. `state.vscdb` also contains composer headers and `cursorDiskKV`. These are different local views, not a proven one-to-one map of complete runs. The JSONL extractor does not parse cloud-run records, so their turn counts and any opaque links to local runs remain unknown. Do not add cloud rows to the JSONL cohort yet.

**Research implication:** At a first look near 60, the Claude candidate pool gains 18 subagent files relative to ≥75, but no additional main session and no new project. Cursor JSONL gains 13 files / 13 unique IDs relative to ≥75. There are enough files for a small first probe, but few long, project-diverse independent cases. Claims of transfer across tasks or harnesses need more cases and outcome evidence. Sessions that finish before 60 can inform the general signal theory; they are not at risk for the first intervention checkpoint.

**Next inventory work:** select a small privacy-screened sample, confirm usable prefix state, identify task type and termination cause, and attach independent deliverable/outcome evidence. Parse the Cursor cloud-run records separately and establish whether their contents or metadata link to JSONL runs before comparing or combining turn counts. Keep multiple checkpoints from one session together and track parent/subagent relationships. Do not count a duplicated Cursor file or many Jev variants as new independent sessions.
