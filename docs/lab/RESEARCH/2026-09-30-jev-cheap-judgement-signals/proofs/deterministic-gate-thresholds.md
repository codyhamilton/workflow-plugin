## Rationale

- **76 vs 75:** Ideal band tops at 75; logging at **76** avoids noise inside the band while matching “signal-worthy once past ~75” in the white paper.
- **85 for Jev:** Keeps model calls off the majority (~84% never left band); still fires **11 turns before** the 96th turn on the 296-call worker (first `band_exit` at 76 → many log rows before 100).
- **100 escalate:** Exact maps `OVER_TURNS` flag — all **8** known >100 workers trip `escalate`.
- **125k / 130k ctx:** Maps OVER_CTX uses **125k** neighborhood; **130k** matches the smoking-gun worker peak (compaction ceiling caveat retained).
- **3MiB / 5MiB JSONL:** Progressive warning before **12.7MB** FAT_JSONL worker; transcript bytes are a cheap hook field when usage parse lags.
