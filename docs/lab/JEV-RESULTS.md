## Accepted facts versus open claims

The narrow mechanics and observed failures above are usable facts **for their stated corpus and instruments**. No test in this register yet establishes that Jev at design/refine/build exit improves delivered quality or safely avoids review. No test yet establishes that a repeated continuation-confidence bar improves useful completion or cost per successful deliverable. Those are the next empirical questions.

When new tests land, add a row here with the source result, population/denominator, conclusion, and limit. Link raw rows or reproducible output. If a later run contradicts a row, keep both results and explain the population or protocol difference; do not silently replace the old result.
